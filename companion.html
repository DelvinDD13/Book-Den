Table of Allomantic Metals

Sixteen base metals in four groups of four. Each pure metal pulls and its alloy pushes. External powers act on the world, internal powers act on you. Kelsier only knew ten of these; the temporal and enhancement sets beyond gold were confirmed after the Final Ascension.

God metals

Atium

Ruin's body. Shows other people's moves a few seconds ahead. Burned against atium, both fighters cancel out.

Lerasium

Preservation's body. Makes the person who burns it a full Mistborn, as strong as the first ones.

Malatium

The "Eleventh Metal," an atium alloy. Shows another person's past self.

House & Court Symbols

Canon marks the symbols described in the books. Derived marks are original designs built from each house's lore where the books never describe a crest.

Canon

House Galathynius · Terrasen

The white stag, Lord of the North, with the unmoving star between its antlers. The same constellation points the way home to Terrasen.

Canon

House Havilliard · Adarlan

The gold wyvern. It is also the second constellation in the sky and sits on Gavin's tomb under the glass castle.

Canon

Mala's Sun-Stag

Cerulean medallion with a white stag crowned in flame. Aelin's fire comes down Brannon and Mala's line.

Canon

The Crochans

Manon's crown of stars, worn when the Crochans answer Terrasen's call in Kingdom of Ash.

Derived

House Whitethorn · Doranelle

Rowan's white-tailed hawk form over a thorn branch. Whitethorn flags fly in Maeve's armada, but the books don't describe the crest.

Derived

House Ashryver · Wendlyn

Mab founded the line and became Deanna, goddess of the moon. The ring is the turquoise-and-gold Ashryver eye.

Derived

Maeve's Cadre

Six warriors bound by blood oath. One drop per sworn member around a single blade.

Derived

The Thirteen · Blackbeak Clan

Ironteeth witches and their wyverns. Manon's coven breaks from the Ironteeth Legion to fight for Aelin.
